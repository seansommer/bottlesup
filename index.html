YOUR FIRST SHIFT

A full belt is a happy belt.

  1. Load a bin. Tap Load bin, then hold ↑. Bottles begin spilling at 42°. Release to hold a gentle trickle, or tilt higher for a bigger dump.
  2. Set your feeder. The slider controls only the short belt. The long belt keeps moving. Lower the dumper before loading the next bin.
  3. Stand and inspect. Tap fallen bottles. Select Reject, then tap a bottle to open its enlarged preview. Check for missing labels, raised/crooked caps, or a low fill. Confirm Reject selected, or Stand & keep. Arrows cycle nearby bottles. Right-click opens inspection on desktop.
  4. Get a closer look. Drag to orbit all the way around the line. Pinch or scroll to zoom; use two fingers or right-drag to move. The Camera panel adds buttons for every adjustment and Reset view.
  5. Build Full Flow. Keep at least 34 good bottles standing to earn 10 seconds of triple points. Fast clean taps build a combo.
  6. Catch a break. Open purple ? bonuses for a helper, stop, stop card, quality sweep, or points. Your first stop card buys 7 seconds.
  7. Finish your pallet. After each juice run, wrap good exits into six-packs and place them on a pallet. This stage is untimed. Auto-pack can handle the rest; incomplete packs stay loose.

Watch downstream: a few bottles tip over again. Fallen bottles or defects reaching the machine lose points. Twelve lost bottles end a competitive run.

V: change view · L: load · Arrow keys: tilt · R: reject · Space: stop · Escape: pause. Turn your phone sideways for a closer view.
